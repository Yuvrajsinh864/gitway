How It Works

From data collection to life-saving action in 5 intelligent steps

1

Data Collection & Integration

Our system continuously gathers data from multiple sources: satellite imagery (NASA, ISRO), weather stations, river gauges, seismic sensors, soil moisture monitors, terrain/DEM data, and historical disaster records. This multi-source approach ensures comprehensive situational awareness even in data-scarce regions.

2

AI/ML Risk Prediction

Advanced machine learning models process the collected data to identify patterns and estimate disaster probability. Our AI analyzes rainfall intensity-duration thresholds, terrain susceptibility, debris-flow runout modeling, and building exposure to generate impact-based forecasts rather than just weather predictions.

3

GIS Risk Mapping & Visualization

Vulnerable regions are visualized on interactive maps with color-coded risk zones (Low/Medium/High/Critical). The system overlays roads, villages, critical infrastructure, hospitals, and evacuation routes to provide complete spatial context for decision-makers.

4

Automated Early Warning System

When risk thresholds are exceeded, the system automatically triggers multi-channel alerts through SMS, email, mobile apps, and social media. Alerts are geo-targeted to affected communities with multilingual support and actionable guidance for immediate response.

5

Resource Optimization & Response

AI algorithms optimize the allocation and deployment of emergency resources—rescue teams, medical supplies, food, water, shelter materials—based on real-time damage assessment, population density, accessibility routes, and priority scoring to maximize lives saved.
